## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a mindful, grounding behavioral economy rooted in circadian rhythms, outdoor presence, and ambient bio-feedback. The aesthetic pairs tactile, editorial botanical serenity with modern, soft-touch ergonomics. It rejects cold algorithmic austerity in favor of warm natural light, vegetable-dyed warmth, and paper textures.

The UI embodies:
- **Atmospheric Vitality:** Compositions breathe with expansive white space, diffuse golden-hour radial glows, and sunlit clarity.
- **Organic Tactility:** Generous pillowy silhouettes, vegetable-parchment surfaces, and micro-relief highlights evoke field journals and sun-warmed stoneware.
- **Low-Arousal Demeanor:** Non-punitive, intentional interactions designed specifically for low eye-strain in bright outdoor sunlight and distraction-free "phone down" rituals.
- **Motif Archetypes:**
  - *EARN:* Sunbursts intertwined with leaf fronds, rendered in honeyed gold tones.
  - *GATE:* Soft luminous apertures, representing transition thresholds into focused presence.
  - *SPIKE:* Prismatic amber and moss sensor-nodes denoting real-time biometrics.
  - *DEBRIEF:* Tactile botanical parchment rolls and deckled summaries for end-of-session reflection.

## Colors

The palette draws entirely from natural elements—sunlight, damp earth, crushed herbs, and unbleached handmade paper. It operates natively in a high-clarity warm light mode suited for daytime visibility.

### Palette Hierarchy
- **Canvas & Backdrops:**
  - `canvas-default`: `#FAF7EE` (Warm unbleached paper)
  - `canvas-recessed`: `#F4EFE0` (Pressed sun-parchment)
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` with a 40% translucent blend onto `#FAF7EE`
- **Primary Accents (Sunlight & Nectar):**
  - `primary-default`: `#D97706` (Warm amber / honey)
  - `primary-bright`: `#F59E0B` (Sunlight gold)
  - `primary-glow`: `#EAB308` (Solar radiant inflection)
- **Natural Accents (Flora & Earth):**
  - `forest-pine`: `#2D5A43` (Deep forest canopy; prime functional actions)
  - `deep-moss`: `#1F3D2E` (Anchor elements & structural accents)
  - `muted-sage`: `#84A98C` (Progress indicators, passive states)
  - `sage-mist`: `#9CAFA0` (Borders, passive divider states)

### Decorative Atmospheric Gradients
- **Solar Halo:** Soft radial gradient from `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)` fading to transparent at 450px radius.
- **Canopy Mist:** Linear gradient from `#FAF7EE` transitioning to `#F4EFE0` at 180deg.

## Typography

Typography balances botanical bookbinding with contemporary app legibility:

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries warmth, literary presence, and cadence. Headings use medium and semi-bold weights with gentle negative tracking to ground the editorial voice of circadian rituals and contemplative debriefs.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides approachable, round, high-legibility geometric forms. Its wide apertures and soft curves complement Playfair Display without competing.
- **Numbers & Readouts:** Metric counters (e.g., minutes in direct sunlight, accumulated presence balance) utilize `Playfair Display` for contemplative scale, paired with uppercase `label-md` units in `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for calm, breath-first readability with deliberately low density. Crowded screens are prohibited.

- **Grid Discipline:** A fluid column system scaling from 4 columns on mobile (360px–599px) with `margin: 1.25rem` and `gutter: 1rem`, to 8 columns on tablet, up to 12 columns pinned at `1040px` max-width on desktop screens.
- **The "Sol Companion" Canvas:** Centered, focused single-card stages for active outdoor sessions, allocating a minimum of `2.5rem` top and bottom margins to promote stillness.
- **Reflow & Safe Areas:** Strict bottom-safe inset protection for gesture bars and thumb-reach zones, keeping interactive triggers grounded within the bottom third of the viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical diffusion, ambient solar warmth, and micro-borders rather than cold digital drop shadows:

- **Tonal Layers:** Surfaces tier naturally against the `#FAF7EE` base using subtle lightness shifts and unbleached parchment overlays (`#FFFFFF` at 85% opacity mixed with warmth).
- **Solar Ambient Shadows:** Shadows are extra-diffused with golden-olive undertones:
  - *Resting Card:* `0 8px 30px -8px rgba(31, 41, 35, 0.05), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.04)`
  - *Floating Interactive (Portal / Gate / Active Timer):* `0 16px 40px -12px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(45, 90, 67, 0.06)`
- **Atmospheric Rim Highlighting:** Interactive containers use a delicate 1px inset border: `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` layered with an outer border of `rgba(132, 169, 140, 0.25)`.
- **Sensory Depth:** Deep modal backdrops use backdrop filters (`blur(12px)`) saturated with a 6% tint of `#FCE7D0`.

## Shapes

The design system uses soft, organic forms inspired by tumbled river stones, seedpods, and natural sun discs:

- **Cards & Enclosures:** Border radii range from `20px` to `32px` (`rounded-xl` to custom `rounded-2xl`), eliminating aggressive corners.
- **Interactive Triggers & Chips:** Full pill geometry (`9999px` / `rounded-full`) to invite touch and imply gentle ease.
- **Motif Enclosures:** Circular and super-elliptical masks that echo natural horizons and sun apertures.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Sun & Earth):** Forest Pine background (`#2D5A43`), white label (`#FAF7EE`), full pill radius (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.875rem 1.75rem`. On press: smooth scale transition (`transform: scale(0.98)`), background shifts to `#1F3D2E`.
- **Secondary (Solar Whisper):** Honey amber tone (`#D97706`), background set to `#FCE7D0` (15% tint), border 1px solid `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No border, `#47554E` label, turning to `#2D5A43` on hover with a faint radial sun glow underneath.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Pill silhouette, `0.375rem 0.875rem`, font `label-md`. 
  - *Sun Tracking:* Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#D97706`, icon motif: Sun disc.
  - *Canopy / Shade:* Background `#E8F0EA`, text `#2D5A43`, icon motif: Duo-leaf.
- **Motif Micro-Badges:** Small circular icon containers (`36px` to `48px`) surrounded by a subtle warm glow (`#FCE7D0`).

### Cards & Motif Enclosures
- **Organic Vessel Card:** Rounded `24px` to `32px`, background `#FFFFFF` blended at 80% opacity over canvas. Border: 1px solid `rgba(156, 175, 160, 0.25)`. Inner padding: `1.5rem` to `2rem`.
- **DEBRIEF Botanical Scroll:** Replaces mechanical summaries with an unbleached parchment treatment (`#F4EFE0`), featuring top and bottom subtle line flourishes and `Playfair Display` for reflective metrics.

### Form Inputs & Selection
- **Input Fields:** Warm paper field fill (`#FFFFFF`), inset boundary `1px solid rgba(132, 169, 140, 0.35)`, corner radius `16px`. Active state transitions to a soft honey rim (`#D97706`) with zero harsh blue outlines.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Curved organic squircle check containers (`6px` radius) and circular radios with moss green check fills (`#2D5A43`) against `#FAF7EE` natural tracks.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **Spaced Flow:** Individual list items are isolated into separate micro-cards rather than separated by single hairline dividers. Spacing between items strictly adheres to `0.75rem`.
- **Leading Accents:** Leading botanical glyphs or circadian timestamps set in `label-sm` eucalyptus tones (`#718277`).

### Specialized Behavioral Modules
- **GATE (Natural Portal):** Concentric circular gradient borders (`#FCE7D0` into `#84A98C`) that expand softly during meditative outdoor lock-in phases.
- **SPIKE (Sensor / Bio-Node):** Glowing ambient pill indicator communicating ultraviolet intensity, light temperature, and cadence in low-contrast ambient gold.
